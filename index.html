Quick Start

  1. Install and launch — a transparent window floats on your desktop
  2. Click the red dots to hit them — new dots spawn randomly
  3. Drag the window edges to resize freely
  4. Watch your stats update in real time at the top

Modes

Normal Mode (default)

Click targets to hit. Reaction time measured from target spawn to click.

Hover Mode (VIP only)

Move your cursor over a target — no click needed. Stats are tracked separately. Toggle in Settings after unlocking VIP.

Stats Bar

MetricDescription
Hits/minHits in the last 60 seconds (sliding window)
AccuracyHit rate in the last 60 seconds
Total HitsLifetime hit counter
ReactionAverage reaction time of recent hits
BestBest records per difficulty (3+ hits required)

Reaction Tester

  1. Click the dot → it fades, text shows Waiting...
  2. After random delay (0.5–7.5s) → dot lights up, text shows Click!
  3. Click again → shows reaction time, best, and 10-trial average
  4. Over 1000ms = timeout, resets automatically

模式

普通模式（默认）

点击目标命中。反应时间从目标生成到点击计算。

悬停模式 (VIP 专属)

光标移到目标上即命中，无需点击。统计数据与普通模式分开记录。解锁 VIP 后在设置中开启。

统计栏

指标说明
命中/分近 60 秒内的命中次数（滑动窗口）
命中率近 60 秒内的命中率
总命中累计命中次数
反应近期命中的平均反应时间
最佳各难度最高记录（需 3 次以上命中）

反应测试

  1. 点击统计栏中的小圆点 → 褪色，显示 等待...
  2. 随机延迟（0.5–7.5 秒）后 → 点亮为目标颜色，显示 点击!
  3. 再次点击 → 显示本次反应时间、最佳和近 10 次平均
  4. 超过 1000ms = 超时，自动重置
